What I work on at iLogitron.

  1. 01

    Change of Land Use Portal

    Revenue Department, Government of Tripura

    • Laravel 12
    • PHP 8
    • MySQL
    • JWT
    • DataTables
    • DomPDF
    • Bootstrap 5
    • Vite

    The state portal where citizens apply to convert land use, and where each application moves from field scrutiny to the sanctioning authority.

    • A 10-stage case workflow across 11 administrative roles, from the Revenue Inspector’s field report to the District Magistrate’s order.
    • Integrations with the state’s other systems: Land Records API (HMAC-SHA256 signed), e-Jami mutation, e-GRAS payments including NEFT for high-value fees, NGDRS property valuation, SWAAGAT single-window sign-on and an SMS gateway.
    • Modules I built or own: the diversion fee calculator, the support ticket desk with chart-driven filtering and exports, MIS and case monitoring views, and an admin impersonation module for support staff.
    • Technical documentation and architecture diagrams for the whole system, written in LaTeX and Excalidraw.

03 / About

Electrical engineering first. Then software, on purpose.

I started in Electrical Engineering at Techno Main Salt Lake, Kolkata. A year and a half in, it was clear that software was what I wanted to do, so I switched to a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Techno College of Engineering, Agartala.

Machine learning is where I started and what I still build on the side: LSTM forecasting, a book summariser, an open-source résumé builder. Along the way I learned web development by shipping it. At Software World I went from trainee to developer intern and built a hospital management system that is in use, and I led development of the live site for the Voluntary Health Association of Tripura.

Today I’m an Associate Software Development Engineer at iLogitron Technologies. Most of my work is Laravel on the backend, on systems that government departments and businesses use every day. I also write the technical documentation and draw the architecture diagrams for them.
